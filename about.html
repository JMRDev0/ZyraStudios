Based in the United States and founded in 2026, Zyra Studios is an independent games studio with a simple philosophy: small studio, big plans. Every project starts small and gets the same care and attention to detail regardless of size — because it deserves it.

The studio's focus is on experiences that carry the spirit of classic interactive entertainment, reimagined with a modern polish. Apogee, the studio's first release, is it's starting point — not its destination.
